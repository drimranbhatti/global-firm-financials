How does your company compare with its listed peers?

Enter your ratios, choose a market and an industry, and see where you rank among listed non-financial companies (fiscal 2017-2019, Compustat Global and North America). Only aggregate percentiles are used, never firm data.

The shaded band is the middle half of peers (25th-75th percentile); the grey line is the median. Rankings are interpolated between deciles. "Better" follows the usual lender and CFO reading: shorter collection and inventory, a shorter cash cycle, higher margins and returns, more cover and less debt. Longer payable days are shown as neutral: they fund you, but they cost supplier goodwill.
